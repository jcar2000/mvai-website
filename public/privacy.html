MVAlert privacy policy

Effective 1 April 2026

Machine Vision AI Pty Ltd (“MVAI”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) operates the MVAlert mobile application and associated services. This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use MVAlert.

1. Information we collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide and improve the service.

Account information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • User role and organisation details

Operational data

  • Alert data, including timestamps, alert type, and event status
  • System-generated incident data
  • Camera-related metadata. Not raw continuous video, unless an alert requires it

Device and usage

  • Device type and operating system
  • App usage, such as interactions and session activity
  • Diagnostic data, such as crash logs and performance data

2. How we use information

  • Provide real-time alerts and incident notifications
  • Enable triage, escalation, and incident management
  • Improve system performance and the accuracy of models
  • Maintain platform security
  • Provide customer support

3. Camera data

MVAlert may process data derived from cameras at customer sites. We primarily process event-based data, not continuous video. Where video or images are used, it is for detecting incidents and for verifying alerts. Processing is under customer direction, for operational purposes.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share data with authorised people in the customer organisation, with offshore monitoring teams for alert review and escalation, with providers who host or run infrastructure, and where the law requires it.

5. International transfers

Data may be processed outside your country, including by offshore monitoring teams. We put safeguards in place so that handling stays consistent with applicable law.

6. Retention

We keep data for as long as needed to provide the service, meet the contract, and comply with the law. Periods vary with the customer agreement.

7. Security

We use access controls, encrypted transmission and storage, and logging of system access. No system can be guaranteed fully secure.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you are, you may be able to access your personal data, ask for correction or deletion, or restrict or object to processing. Use the contact below. Account deletion for the app is described on the MVAlert support page.

9. Third parties

MVAlert may connect to client platforms and communication tools. Those services have their own privacy policies.

10. Children

MVAlert is not for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted here with a revised effective date.
