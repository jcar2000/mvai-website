Delete your account and data

App: MVAlert. Developer: Machine Vision AI Pty Ltd.

How to request deletion

  1. Email support@mvai.com.au with the subject “MVAlert Account Deletion Request”.
  2. Include the email address or username on the account.
  3. We reply to confirm you own the account.
  4. Once that is confirmed, we delete the account and the data listed below within 30 days, and email you when it is done.

Data that is deleted

  • Account profile: name, email address, phone number
  • Login credentials
  • App settings, preferences, and notification subscriptions
  • Device identifiers and push notification tokens
  • Usage and diagnostic data linked to the account

Data that is kept

  • Security and audit logs
  • Anonymised or aggregated analytics

See the privacy policy for how data is collected and used.
